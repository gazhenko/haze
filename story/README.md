# Haze: the full game

> **Draft for discussion.** Nothing in this directory is in the game yet. It contains spoilers for Haze 0.4.0 and for the planned story.

Haze 0.4.0 is one building: the bathhouse on the headland. This directory plans the full game — about forty rooms across an island — before anything is built.

## The pitch

You row into a fog bank carrying a sealed letter for “E. Vale, Keeper, Morrow Baths.” The island of Morrow was emptied twenty years ago. It is still raining there, it is still the same evening, and every clock reads 7:42. You finish the island’s evening rounds one region at a time, and each region you finish gets its morning back.

## One constraint

The shipped bathhouse becomes Act I with its puzzles unchanged, and both of Elias’s letters stay word for word. Every line in them still holds under the larger story; it reads differently the second time.

## Open decisions

Each has a recommendation; the rest of these documents assume it.

1. **Mara’s fate.** *Recommended:* she lived and left on the last boat. The passenger list, the telegraph and her unopened letters then become a ladder of reveals. *Alternatives:* lost at sea with the ferry, or left ambiguous to the end — though forty rooms need something to build toward.
2. **Who you are.** *Recommended:* an anonymous courier carrying Mara’s letter. *Alternatives:* explicitly her grandchild, or no letter at all, as in 0.4.0.
3. **Elias’s fate.** *Recommended:* he rows across as you arrive; you pass his lantern in the haze in the first minute. *Alternative:* you find him asleep in the lamp-room chair (stronger, heavier, and it needs a character model).
4. **A second ending.** *Recommended:* a hidden choice to wind the lighthouse clockwork instead of putting out the light. You stay as keeper, the evening holds, and the last line is the first letter’s: *It has been raining here for a long time.*
5. **Era and place.** *Assumed:* a North Atlantic island closed in the 1950s, about twenty years before you arrive. This sets the telegraph, the electric master clock, the steam pumps and the look of everything.

## Working names

All placeholders: **Morrow** (the island; an old word for “tomorrow”), the **Kittiwake** (the last ferry), **Ardmouth** (the mainland harbour), the **Morrow Spring Company** (the owners).
